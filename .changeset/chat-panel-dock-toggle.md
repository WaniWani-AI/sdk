`data-panel-dockable="true"` adds a header button that lets the visitor move the open panel between the centered modal and the right-hand sidebar; `data-panel-layout` is where it starts. Off by default, so nothing changes without the attribute. New wording keys `launcher.toSidebar` and `launcher.toModal`, new part `ww-layout-toggle`, new `onEvent` event `panel.layout.changed`.
